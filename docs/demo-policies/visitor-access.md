# Visitor Access and Conduct

**Document ID:** DEMO-ADM-001
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This fictional policy sets administrative expectations for visitors in a healthcare facility. It is not a substitute for local safety, privacy, or infection-prevention instructions.

## Check-in and identification
Visitors use the designated check-in point and follow staff instructions for identification, registration, and access areas. A visitor badge or escort is used where required by local procedure. Access may be limited during an incident or operational disruption.

## Privacy and conduct
Visitors must respect posted restrictions and the privacy of patients and staff. Photography, recording, or access to records is allowed only through the facility's approved authorization process. Staff route privacy concerns to the designated contact.

## Emergency coordination
During an incident, visitors follow instructions from the designated response team and use the communication plan described in the Emergency Code and Incident Communication policy. Business continuity arrangements are described in Backup and Disaster Recovery.

## Review
Administration reviews this policy after a significant incident or scheduled review and removes outdated copies from circulation.
