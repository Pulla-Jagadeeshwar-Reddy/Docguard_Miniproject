# Standard Precautions and Sharps Injury Response

**Document ID:** DEMO-IC-001
**Owner:** Infection Prevention Team
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This example policy describes a basic workflow for reducing avoidable exposure to blood and body fluids. It is fictional demonstration content, not a clinical protocol. A real facility must replace it with approved local procedures.

## Standard precautions
Staff should perform hand hygiene at the required points of care, select personal protective equipment based on the anticipated exposure, and dispose of sharps immediately in approved puncture-resistant containers. Used needles must not be routinely recapped, bent, or separated by hand. Reusable equipment must follow the facility's validated cleaning and reprocessing procedure.

## Sharps injury response
After a suspected sharps injury, stop the task safely, perform the locally approved first aid, and promptly contact the designated occupational-health or emergency pathway. Report the incident through the facility's incident reporting process. Clinical evaluation, testing, and any post-exposure intervention must be determined by qualified clinicians using current facility policy and applicable guidance.
