# Backup and Disaster Recovery

**Document ID:** DEMO-EP-002
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This fictional administrative policy describes ownership of backup and recovery planning. It does not specify real recovery times, clinical continuity instructions, or technical configuration.

## Backup ownership
System owners maintain an inventory of critical services and their approved backup responsibilities. Backup jobs and access to backup media are monitored through the facility's documented information-security process.

## Recovery planning
The continuity owner records dependencies, escalation contacts, recovery priorities, and exercise outcomes in an approved plan. Recovery tests use a controlled environment and do not expose real patient information.

## Incident coordination
During a disruption, the continuity owner coordinates with the Emergency Code and Incident Communication policy. Visitor-facing instructions are coordinated with Visitor Access and Conduct. These references form a deliberate cycle for the dependency-graph demonstration.
