**Document ID:** DEMO-IC-003
**Status:** Synthetic training example

## Purpose
This fictional quick reference repeats the local hand-hygiene reminder for clinical staff. It is a teaching example, not a clinical protocol. Follow the facility's approved infection-prevention guidance.

## When to clean hands
Clean hands at the locally defined points before and after patient contact, before a clean task, after exposure risk, and after contact with the patient's surroundings. Use the hand-cleaning method approved for the situation and the facility.

## Safe workflow
Keep hand-cleaning supplies available, follow product instructions, and report empty dispensers or equipment problems. Gloves do not replace hand hygiene. If hands are visibly soiled or a specific exposure requires a different method, follow the approved local procedure.
