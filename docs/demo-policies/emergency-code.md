# Emergency Code and Incident Communication

**Document ID:** DEMO-EP-001
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This fictional policy describes a communication framework for facility incidents. It does not define clinical response steps or replace local emergency plans.

## Activation and notification
Use the facility's approved channel and terminology to notify the designated response team. Share the location, type of incident, and known safety concerns without speculation. If the primary communication system is unavailable, use the documented backup channel.

## Coordination
The incident lead records key decisions, assigns a communications contact, and coordinates with affected departments. Staff follow their role-specific instructions and avoid unverified announcements.

## Visitors and continuity
The responsible team coordinates visitor instructions with the Visitor Access and Conduct policy. Technology or service interruptions are coordinated with the Backup and Disaster Recovery policy.

## Review
The safety team reviews exercises and incidents, records follow-up actions, and updates the approved plan through document control.
