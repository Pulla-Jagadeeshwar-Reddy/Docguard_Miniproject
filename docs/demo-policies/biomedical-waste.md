# Biomedical Waste Segregation

**Document ID:** DEMO-WM-001
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This fictional policy explains how a facility assigns responsibility for waste handling. It is an educational example; use the locally approved waste-management procedure and applicable guidance.

## Segregation at the point of use
Staff place waste into the locally designated, labeled container at the point where it is generated. Do not sort waste by hand after disposal. Follow container limits and closure instructions, and keep incompatible waste streams separate.

## Collection and storage
Only trained, authorized personnel collect and move sealed containers along approved routes. Report leaks, damaged packaging, missing labels, or overflowing containers promptly. The designated team maintains collection and handoff records.

## Training and acknowledgment
Departments ensure that assigned personnel know the current local procedure and acknowledge the active policy version in DocuGuard.
