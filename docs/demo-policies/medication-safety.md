# Medication Safety and Independent Checks

**Document ID:** DEMO-MED-001
**Status:** Synthetic training example
**Review interval:** 12 months

## Purpose
This fictional policy describes administrative safeguards for a facility's approved medication workflow. It does not give clinical or dosing advice.

## Preparation and verification
Use only the facility-approved system and current authorized order. Confirm the relevant patient and medication details using the local procedure. Independent checks are required only where the approved protocol specifies them; they must be performed by a qualified second person rather than documented as a proxy.

## Storage and access
Store medications in approved locations with access limited to authorized personnel. Follow local temperature, security, labeling, and inventory controls. Report damaged, expired, missing, or mismatched items through the designated process.

## Incident reporting
Pause and escalate an uncertain or discrepant order through the approved clinical chain. Report errors and near misses promptly so the safety team can review contributing system factors.

## Training and acknowledgment
Assigned personnel read the current approved version and acknowledge that version. This policy does not replace training or clinical judgment.
